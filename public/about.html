SHAKTHI ENTERPRISES / BENGALURU

One manufacturing partner.
A connected way of working.

Shakthi Enterprises brings tool design, mould development, plastic injection moulding and assembly into one manufacturing conversation. Established in 2006 and based in Bengaluru, the company works across Tooling operations and Production operations.

Our work starts with understanding what the component needs to do. The drawing, sample, application, material and quantity become the common reference for engineering, tooling, production and inspection. The goal is a part that can be made consistently and assessed against clear criteria.

01 / ENGINEERED WITH PURPOSE

Tooling operations

Connects the engineering brief with mould development, trials, refinements and production-machine requirements. Tool identity, compatibility and maintenance remain part of the conversation.

02 / ENGINEERED WITH PURPOSE

Production operations

Connects approved components and tools with machine planning, shift output, quality checks, accepted stock, packing and delivery.

03 / ENGINEERED WITH PURPOSE

A shared standard

The same component revision, ownership, quality requirement and delivery commitment should follow the work from the first enquiry to the finished shipment.
