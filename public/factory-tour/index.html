Inside the press.

ROOM 03 / 08
Raw material store

Choose the resin.

The drawing specifies more than a colour. The clips must flex, the walls must hold their shape and the finish must suit the customer’s sample. In the store, resin grade and batch travel with the job.

PP & PPCP

Polypropylene grades suit light, flexible parts. Copolymer grades offer options where impact resistance matters.

ABS & polycarbonate

ABS is a candidate for rigid housings with a good surface finish. Polycarbonate is considered when impact strength or clarity matters.

Nylon & POM

These engineering plastics are candidates for sliding and load-bearing features. Moisture exposure and dimensional requirements guide the choice.

Colour masterbatch

Concentrated pigment pellets are blended with the base resin to match a colour sample.

Plastic granulator

Cutters reduce sorted sprues and runners into regrind. Reuse depends on the resin and the customer’s material specification.

ROOM 04 / 08
Tool room I

Cut the shape in steel.

The mould determines the shape of every housing. In the first tooling bay, machinists turn the drawing into steel faces, holes and profiles.

Milling machine

A rotating cutter removes steel from the clamped workpiece to create flats, slots and pockets.

Centre lathe

The workpiece rotates against a cutting tool to form cylindrical pins, sleeves and other round tool elements.

Pillar drill

A guided spindle cuts holes in tool plates for fasteners and other fittings.

ROOM 05 / 08
Tool room II

Make the halves meet.

The next bay focuses on the surfaces that meet when the tool closes. A small mismatch here can leave a visible line on every finished cover.

Wire EDM

Electrical discharges between a travelling wire and conductive steel cut intricate through-profiles in a tool insert.

Surface grinder

An abrasive wheel takes fine passes across steel faces to establish flat, mating surfaces.

ROOM 06 / 08
Quality room

Check the fit.

Under the inspection light, the housing meets its drawing again. The inspector checks the outside face, measures the critical features and tries the clips against the mating component.

Vernier caliper

Measures accessible outside widths, inside spans and depths against the part drawing.

Micrometer & dial indicator

A micrometer measures a selected thickness or diameter. A dial indicator shows small changes relative to a reference position.

ROOM 07 / 08
Ready stock

Ready for the next assembly.

Approved housings are counted and packed with their faces protected. Cartons are identified by part and batch, then moved to the dispatch lane for collection.

Finished components

The packing method follows the geometry. Ribs and clips need clearance so that neighbouring parts do not rub or catch in transit.
