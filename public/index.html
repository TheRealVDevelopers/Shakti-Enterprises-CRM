BEFORE WE MAKE IT

Let’s get the
important details right.

What do you need to start a mould-making discussion?

A drawing, 3D model or sample is a useful starting point. Share the application, critical dimensions, material preference, target quantity, mating parts and intended delivery date.

Can we discuss a part before the material is decided?

Yes. Start with what the component needs to do and the conditions it will face. The specific material grade and required validation are agreed before production approval.

How are production and inspection connected?

The job card links the approved component to its machine, mould and material. Actual output enters inspection; only accepted stock becomes available for dispatch.

Are the new images photographs of Shakthi’s facility?

The manufacturing visuals are labelled AI-created illustrations. Actual product, machine and employee photographs can be uploaded privately to the CRM records by authorised users.
