PP / Polypropylene

Lightweight housings & covers

A candidate family for battery cases, general housings, caps and protective components. Select the grade against impact, temperature, chemical exposure, joining and UV requirements.

What we review with you

Service temperature, impact or static load, dimensional tolerance, surface finish, environmental exposure, assembly method and expected life. We also review drying, shrinkage and processing requirements from the material supplier.

Fittings & protective parts
