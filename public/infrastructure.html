Capacity is more
than a tonnage number.

Clamping force is one part of machine selection. The mould’s dimensions, tie-bar clearance, mounting, opening stroke and ejection requirements must fit. Shot capacity, plasticising capability, material, projected area and the approved process window also influence the choice.
