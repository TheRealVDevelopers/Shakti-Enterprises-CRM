COMPONENT / CASE

The outer case

The structural envelope.

The moulded case houses the battery assembly and establishes the overall dimensions and mounting interface. Wall consistency, impact behaviour and material compatibility are reviewed against the customer’s design.

Design & inspection focus

Check the envelope, wall transitions, material grade, mounting features and the assembly’s required tests.

COMPONENT / LID

The top cover

A precise joining surface.

The lid closes the plastic enclosure and carries interfaces specified by the battery designer. The case-to-cover joint must suit the approved assembly and sealing process.

Design & inspection focus

Check the joint geometry, cover flatness, process allowance and matching case revision.

COMPONENT / PARTITIONS

Internal partitions

Six compartments, clearly defined.

The illustrative case contains six compartments. Moulded internal walls define their separation and must align with the rest of the approved assembly.

Design & inspection focus

Check partition position, wall thickness, tool filling and distortion against the drawing.

COMPONENT / TERMINALS

Terminal interfaces

The connection starts with the fit.

Moulded openings, seats or surrounding features locate the customer’s electrical connections. This page illustrates the plastic interface; it does not claim that Shakthi manufactures conductive posts or complete batteries.

Design & inspection focus

Check the interface drawing, insert requirements, clearances and assembly process with the battery designer.

COMPONENT / VENTS

Vent & fill interfaces

Openings with a defined purpose.

The customer’s battery design determines the vent and fill arrangements. The moulded cover must reproduce the approved seats and openings consistently.

Design & inspection focus

Validate the complete battery’s venting and safety requirements with the responsible designer; the illustration is not a functional vent specification.

How the mould is developed ↗

COMPONENT / RIBS

Ribs & mounting features

Support, without unnecessary mass.

Ribs and locating features support the moulded component and help position it in its installation. Balanced transitions, radii and draft help the tool release the part while managing local stress.

Design & inspection focus

Check rib proportions, sink risk, mounting load, clearances and the tool’s ejection requirements.

How the mould is developed ↗
