BUILT AROUND THE FIELD

Small components.
Practical responsibilities.

Agricultural components are shaped by use: how they connect, how they are handled, how long they remain outdoors and what they encounter in service. We discuss the component requirement before selecting a material or committing a tool.

01 / ENGINEERED WITH PURPOSE

Irrigation connections

Candidate applications include connectors, adapters, caps and protective fittings. Mating dimensions, sealing geometry and any pressure duty must be specified and validated.

02 / ENGINEERED WITH PURPOSE

Crop & nursery support

Candidate moulded parts include support clips, tray concepts and protective items. Handling, cleaning, sunlight and expected service life influence the brief.

03 / ENGINEERED WITH PURPOSE

Equipment protection

Covers, guards, knobs and brackets can support agricultural equipment. Fit, vibration, impact and environmental exposure determine the design requirements.

MATERIAL MEETS ENVIRONMENT

Sun. Water. Handling.
Plan for all three.

Outdoor use can require UV-stabilised grades. Moisture, fertilisers, cleaning agents and temperature cycles add further questions. A resin family alone is not evidence that a component is suitable for a pressure system, drinking water or chemical contact.

We review the approved grade, drawing and required validation with the customer. Any regulated or certified use requires the appropriate supporting evidence for the complete product.
