BEFORE WE BEGIN

A few useful answers.

Have a specific technical requirement?
Talk to our team ↗

What should I share for a quotation?

A drawing, 3D model or sample is a good starting point. Include your expected quantity, preferred material, colour or finish, critical dimensions and delivery requirement. If you have an existing mould, share its details too.

Can you develop the mould as well as the component?

Yes. Our capabilities include part and mould design, tooling, mould trials, injection moulding, finishing, assembly and packing. We can also discuss mould repairs and modifications.

Do you support short runs and larger production volumes?

Yes. We welcome prototype, short-run and high-volume enquiries. The production plan and quotation depend on your part, tooling, material and quantity.

Can you process recycled materials?

We process virgin, recycled and regrind materials. Suitability depends on the part application and its quality requirements; our team can discuss this with you.
